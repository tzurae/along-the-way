# Third-Party Notices

## airportsdata

The generated airport search data in `apps/web/src/airport-data.generated.ts` is derived from [mborsetti/airportsdata](https://github.com/mborsetti/airportsdata) version 20260905, pinned to commit [`47ae282a8491758e6dafe28d4139539e561b54d5`](https://github.com/mborsetti/airportsdata/commit/47ae282a8491758e6dafe28d4139539e561b54d5). Only IATA code, airport name, city, ISO country code, latitude, longitude, and IANA time zone are retained. The upstream project notes that its time-zone data was originally sourced from [TimeZoneDB](https://timezonedb.com).
